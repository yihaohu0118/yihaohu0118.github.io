Hello! I'm Yihao Hu (胡益豪), a Ph.D. student in Artificial Intelligence at Westlake University, advised by Prof. Tao Lin. I received my undergraduate degree in Computer Science from Hainan University.

I am currently a research intern at Ant Group. Previously, I worked with Meituan's LongCat team on native multimodal LLMs and with Alibaba Amap on AI agents and LLM alignment.

My research focuses on agents and multimodal intelligence — how models learn from interaction, reason over video, and turn experience into reusable knowledge.
